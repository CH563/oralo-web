The Oralo app

Oralo is made by Chenliwen. Scripts, original drafts, pronunciation notes, reading preferences and rehearsal records are stored in the app’s local storage, including local snapshots and recovery copies. Oralo does not upload your scripts or microphone audio to a developer-operated server.

No Oralo account is required. The app contains no advertising or analytics SDK and does not track you across apps or websites.

Your voice, used to follow your words

Voice following uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition after you grant the required permissions. Audio and recognition results are processed temporarily to find your place. Oralo does not save microphone recordings or full speech transcripts.

Rehearsal records can include duration, progress and events such as pauses or returns to the script, together with a local snapshot of the script. They remain on your device. You can use manual reading without microphone access.

Floating voice following may continue while its Picture-in-Picture window is active. Closing the window stops that session. Fixed-pace floating prompts do not use the microphone. Other apps you use for recording or calls have their own data practices.

Downloads, sharing and backups

Apple speech models and system voices may need to download before use. Files you choose from iCloud Drive or another provider may also require internet access. When on-device recognition is unavailable, Oralo offers manual reading rather than sending your audio to a cloud recognizer.

Exports and diagnostic reports leave Oralo only when you choose to share them. Diagnostics contain system information, performance counters and event metadata, not script text, pronunciation notes or full transcripts. Check any message or attachment before sharing it.

Your operating system may include app data in device backups. Apple, file providers and services you choose to share with handle their own data under their policies.

Deleting your local data

Use Settings → On-device data to remove rehearsal records or all local app data. Deletion also clears the corresponding local recovery copies so they cannot restore what you removed. You can also delete individual scripts from the library.

Deletion inside Oralo cannot erase copies you exported elsewhere or existing operating-system backups. Restoring an older device backup may bring back data from that backup.

This website

The website is separate from the app and no longer accepts email registrations. If you previously registered for Oralo launch updates, your email, optional use case, selected language, registration date and consent version remain in private storage for those communications. These records are not published, sold or used for unrelated advertising. They are removed when no longer needed or when you request deletion.

To access or delete an existing registration, contact wonderful563@gmail.com from the registered email address. Existing authenticated deletion requests remain supported. This browser stores your language preference locally; the home page clears its former registration credential from session storage.

The online speech tools do not upload or save your scripts. Their text stays in the current page and is cleared when you leave or refresh. Speech preview passes text to the voice you explicitly choose and only lists voices the browser reports as local. That flag is not an independent audit of browser or operating-system processing. The homepage pronunciation demo uses the browser’s voice service, which may use a network-based voice. The website does not use your microphone.

We do not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Vercel hosts the site and may process network addresses and request metadata to operate and protect it. The legacy deletion endpoint temporarily uses a network-address hash to limit abuse.

Support, purchases and updates

If you contact us, we receive the address and information you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Please do not send a private script, recording or diagnostic report unless you intend to share it. Public GitHub issues are visible to everyone.

App purchases and payments are handled by Apple. Oralo does not receive your payment card information. This policy will be updated if these practices change.

For privacy requests or questions, see our support page. You can inspect the iOS source under the MIT License on GitHub.

Oralo App 的本机数据

Oralo 由 Chenliwen 开发。讲稿、原稿、拼音与音标、阅读偏好和排练记录保存在 App 的本机存储中，包括历史快照和恢复副本。Oralo 不会把讲稿或麦克风音频上传到开发者运营的服务器。

App 无需 Oralo 账号，不包含广告或分析 SDK，也不跨 App 或网站追踪你。

用声音定位你正在讲的内容

取得必要权限后，语音跟随使用 Apple 的设备端语音识别。音频和识别结果仅临时用于定位原稿，Oralo 不保存麦克风录音或完整转写。

排练记录可能包括时长、进度、暂停、接稿等事件，以及本机讲稿快照。你可以不授权麦克风，使用手动阅读。

语音小窗处于活动状态时，跟随可能在后台继续；关闭小窗会结束该次收音。定速小窗不使用麦克风。用于录像或通话的其他 App 遵循各自的数据政策。

下载、分享与系统备份

Apple 语音模型和系统声音可能需要先联网下载；从 iCloud 云盘或其他文件提供方选择文件，也可能需要联网。设备端识别不可用时，Oralo 提供手动阅读，不会改用云端转写。

只有你主动分享时，导出内容或诊断报告才会离开 Oralo。诊断报告包含系统信息、性能计数和事件元数据，不含稿件正文、注音或完整转写。发送前请检查附件和消息。

操作系统可能将 App 数据纳入设备备份。Apple、文件提供方以及你选择的分享服务，分别按照自己的政策处理数据。

删除你的本机数据

在「设置 → 本机数据」中可删除排练记录或清空本机数据。删除会同时清理对应恢复副本，防止副本重新恢复已删除内容。你也可以在稿库中单独删除稿件。

App 内删除无法擦除已导出到其他位置的副本或已有的系统备份。恢复旧设备备份时，可能重新带回该备份中的数据。

官网数据

官网与 App 分开运行，现已停止接受邮箱登记。若你曾登记接收 Oralo 上线通知，邮箱、选填场景、所选语言、登记时间和同意版本仍保存在私有存储中，仅用于原先约定的通知。这些记录不公开、不出售，也不用于无关广告；不再需要或收到删除请求时会予以删除。

如需查看或删除已有登记，请使用登记邮箱联系 wonderful563@gmail.com。已有的凭据验证删除请求仍可使用。浏览器在本机保存语言偏好；首页会清理会话存储中的旧登记凭据。

在线演讲工具不上传或保存讲稿，文字只保留在当前页面，离开或刷新后清空。演讲稿试听会把文字交给你明确选择的语音服务，只列出浏览器报告为本地的声音；这一标记不代表对浏览器或操作系统处理过程的独立审计。首页读音演示使用浏览器语音服务，其中的声音可能需要联网。官网不使用麦克风。

我们未添加广告追踪或分析脚本。网站由 Vercel 托管，服务商可能为运行和保护网站处理网络地址及请求元数据。保留的删除接口会临时使用网络地址哈希限制滥用。

联系支持、购买与更新

你联系我们时，我们会收到你主动提供的地址和内容，仅用于处理请求。请只分享你愿意提供的稿件、录音或诊断报告。GitHub 公开问题对所有人可见。

App 购买和支付由 Apple 处理，Oralo 不接收银行卡信息。数据处理方式发生变化时，本页会相应更新。

隐私请求请通过支持页面联系我们。iOS 源码采用 MIT 许可证，可在 GitHub 查看。
