Open an issue ↗
How do I start?

Download Oralo from the App Store. Create a script, paste your text or import a TXT/Markdown file. Check the estimated speaking time, add any pronunciation hints, then choose Start speaking. The preparation check helps you confirm speech permissions and models before you begin.

How do I return after going off script?

Speak a distinctive nearby part of the script to help Oralo find your place. If needed, reveal the reading controls, choose Pick up and select a sentence. Manual controls remain available. Accuracy depends on the words, background sound and system recognition.

How does the floating prompt work?

Open your script’s options and choose Floating prompt. Choose fixed pace or start voice following inside Oralo, then open Picture in Picture and switch apps. Drag or resize the window using the system gestures; text size is adjusted in Oralo. The window uses a solid background. Compatibility with recording and calling apps can vary by device, system and app. If microphone input is interrupted, your place is held; return to Oralo to restart voice following or choose fixed pace.

Can I use it offline?

Editing and manual reading work with local data. Voice following needs a supported on-device speech model downloaded in advance. System models, voices and files from cloud providers may require internet access. If recognition is unavailable, use manual reading.

How do pronunciation hints work?

Select a word while preparing, then choose Pronunciation. Review and save Chinese pinyin or US English IPA. Listen plays the original English word with a system voice; editing the IPA does not change the synthesized pronunciation. Saved hints remain visible while presenting. Rehearsal review helps you check words yourself; it does not grade pronunciation.

What does the app cost?

Oralo is available on the App Store. One download includes all features, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. Prices and promotions can vary by storefront; check the price shown in your region before downloading.

Where are my scripts? Can I recover deleted data?

Scripts, reading hints and rehearsal records are stored locally. Manage them in Settings → On-device data. Removal also clears the corresponding recovery copies. Oralo has no developer-hosted script backup. Copies you export and operating-system backups are separate; deletion inside Oralo does not erase those copies.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Oralo supports iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 18 or later, with light and dark appearances. The interface follows your app language: English, Simplified Chinese or Traditional Chinese. Speech availability depends on your device, language and installed system models.

How do I report a problem?

Include your Oralo version/build, device model, iOS version, the steps you took and what you expected to happen. For floating prompts, include the other app and whether recording or a call was active. A short non-sensitive example is more useful than your entire private script. Do not post personal information in a public issue.

提交问题 ↗
怎样开始使用？

先在 App Store 下载 Oralo。新建、粘贴或导入 TXT／Markdown 稿件，查看预计演讲时长，按需添加注音，再点「开讲」。准备检查可帮助你提前确认权限和设备端语音模型。

自由发挥后，怎样接回原稿？

读出附近一段有辨识度的原稿，帮助 Oralo 定位。必要时显示演讲控制栏，点「接稿」并选择一句，再继续。手动控制始终可用；跟随效果受内容、噪声和系统识别影响。

小窗怎样使用？

从稿件选项进入「小窗提词」。选择定速，或先在 Oralo 内启动语音跟随，再开启画中画并切换 App。可用系统手势移动或缩放窗口，文字大小在 Oralo 内调整。小窗使用实色背景。与录像、通话 App 的兼容性会因设备、系统和 App 而异；收音中断时保留原位置，返回 Oralo 后可重新启动语音或改用定速。

可以离线使用吗？

编辑和手动阅读使用本机数据。语音跟随需要提前下载设备支持的设备端模型；系统模型、声音和云端文件可能需要联网。识别不可用时可使用手动阅读。

怎样添加拼音和音标？

备稿时选中字词，再点「注音」，检查并保存中文拼音或美式英文 IPA。试听使用系统声音朗读英文原词；编辑音标不会改变合成发音。演讲时保留读音提示。复盘提供手动核对入口，不进行发音评分。

App 如何收费？

Oralo 已在 App Store 上架。下载后即可使用全部功能，没有订阅或 App 内购买。不同地区的价格及优惠可能不同，下载前请以所在地区 App Store 显示为准。

稿件存在哪里？删除后能找回吗？

讲稿、读音提示和排练记录均保存在本机，可在「设置 → 本机数据」管理。删除会同时清理对应恢复副本；Oralo 没有开发者托管的讲稿云备份。你导出的副本和系统备份独立存在，App 内删除无法擦除它们。

支持哪些设备和语言？

支持 iOS／iPadOS 18 及以上的 iPhone 和 iPad，支持浅色与深色。界面根据 App 语言显示英文、简体中文或繁体中文；语音可用性取决于设备、语言和已安装的系统模型。

怎样反馈问题？

请提供 Oralo 版本／构建号、设备型号、iOS 版本、操作步骤和预期结果。小窗问题请同时说明另一个 App，以及当时是否在录像或通话。简短且不含隐私的示例即可，不需要提供整篇私人讲稿；请勿在公开问题中填写个人信息。
