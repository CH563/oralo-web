← Back to homeFLOATING PROMPT

Switch apps. Keep your place.

The development app can place your script in a Picture-in-Picture window on iPhone or iPad, so the next line can stay visible while you record or go live.

Set a steady pace

Fixed pace: set a scroll speed and start after a 3-second countdown. Fixed pace uses no microphone.

Start voice following in Oralo

Voice following: start voice following in Oralo, then open the window and switch apps.

If the microphone is interrupted

If recording or a call in another app interrupts the microphone, the window holds your place. Return to Oralo to restart voice following, or switch to fixed pace.

Keep your reading hints close

Saved pinyin and IPA stay with their words. Change text size in Oralo; returning to Oralo pauses the window.

Compatibility and tested scope

Floating prompts have been tested with system Camera recording. Behavior can vary on other devices and in calling apps. This does not mean every device is covered, or that every recording or calling app is supported.

How voice following works ↗

A few things to know.

How do I start voice following in the floating window?

Start voice following inside Oralo, then open Picture-in-Picture and switch apps. Fixed pace does not use the microphone and is not a substitute for voice following.

Can I use it with the system Camera?

Floating prompts have been tested with system Camera recording. In other recording or calling apps, or on other devices, behavior can vary by device, system, and the app in use.

What if the microphone is interrupted?

The window holds your current place. Return to Oralo to restart voice following, or continue with fixed-pace scrolling.
