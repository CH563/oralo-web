A few things to know.

Can I download Oralo yet?

Oralo is in development and testing, ahead of public release. Join the waitlist for testing opportunities and launch updates. The site shows development builds, and features may still change.

How does voice following work?

Oralo matches on-device speech recognition to your script and highlights your place as you speak. It waits during pauses. If it clearly recognizes original wording, it may try to return to the same sentence or a nearby later line. You can also select a line yourself; your choice takes priority. Performance depends on your environment, device, and language, and is still being tested.

Can I automatically return after going off script?

We focus on returning to the same sentence or nearby lines, and do not promise accurate jumps across the whole script. Noise, distance, and other voices can affect recognition. You can select a line yourself whenever you need to.

Which devices and languages are supported?

The current development app supports iPhone and iPad on iOS / iPadOS 18 or later. The interface is available in English, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese, with Chinese pinyin and English IPA hints. Voice following requires a supported device, language, and installed system speech model.

What can I revisit after a rehearsal?

You can review duration, pauses, returns to the script, and supported quiet or repeated-passage cues, then revisit the original line, edit your draft, or check a word. These are review aids, not a speaking score or an automatic pronunciation assessment.

Can I use Oralo offline?

Script editing and reading use local data. Voice following needs a supported system speech model already downloaded to your device; the first download of a model or voice may require the internet. When on-device recognition is unavailable, manual reading remains available, and the app will not send audio to a cloud recognizer instead.

Can I use the floating prompt with Camera or a call?

The development app offers Picture-in-Picture prompting with fixed-pace scrolling or voice following. Fixed pace needs no microphone; voice following starts inside Oralo. If recording or a call in another app interrupts the microphone, your place is held. Floating prompts have been tested with system Camera recording. Behavior can vary by device, system, and the other app, including other recording or calling apps.
