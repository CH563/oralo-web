A few things to know.

Where can I download Oralo?

Oralo is available on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. One download includes all features, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases. See the App Store for the current price in your region.

How does voice following work?

Oralo matches on-device speech recognition to your script and highlights your place as you speak. It waits during pauses. If it clearly recognizes original wording, it may try to return to the same sentence or a nearby later line. You can also select a line yourself; your choice takes priority. Performance depends on your environment, device, and language.

Can I automatically return after going off script?

Oralo can look for the same sentence or nearby lines when it clearly recognizes the original wording. It does not promise accurate jumps across the whole script. Noise, distance, and other voices can affect recognition. You can select a line yourself whenever you need to.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Oralo supports iPhone and iPad on iOS / iPadOS 18 or later. A separate Mac test preview is available for macOS 15 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. The iOS interface supports English, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese; the Mac preview supports English and Simplified Chinese. Both include Chinese pinyin and English IPA hints. Voice following needs a supported device, language and installed system speech model.

What can I revisit after a rehearsal?

You can review duration, pauses, returns to the script, and supported quiet or repeated-passage cues, then revisit the original line, edit your draft, or check a word. These are review aids, not a speaking score or an automatic pronunciation assessment.

Can I use Oralo offline?

Script editing and reading use local data. Voice following needs a supported system speech model already downloaded to your device; the first download of a model or voice may require the internet. When on-device recognition is unavailable, manual reading remains available, and the app will not send audio to a cloud recognizer instead.

Can I use the floating prompt with Camera or a call?

Open your script → (…) → Floating prompt. Fixed pace needs no microphone; voice following starts inside Oralo. Camera photo preview was checked on iPhone 15 Pro / iOS 18.1. Separate feedback confirmed following during recording and normal recorded speech, without a complete device/app list. Other recording, livestream and calling apps need a check on your own setup.
