How do I use it?

Paste a script, choose the main language, a browser-reported local voice, and a relative rate, then press Play. Stop to make changes and listen again.

A reference, not a rehearsal.

Short gaps may occur between passages. This preview uses one selected voice and does not measure your speaking time. You control every start; stopping, editing, or leaving the foreground never resumes playback automatically.

Before you listen

Does a local voice guarantee fully offline use?

No independent audit is implied. This tool only lists voices the browser reports as local and does not fall back to remote voices. This page does not control browser or system service internals. Installing a voice may require an internet connection.

Is my script saved or uploaded?

This site does not save your script or send it to site APIs, analytics, or third-party scripts. Text is passed to the selected browser speech synthesis service. This is not an audit of all processing performed by your system.

What if no local voice is available?

The browser may not have loaded or reported a matching voice. Reload the list, or check speech voices in system settings and try again. Without a match, playback stays unavailable. There is no automatic remote fallback.

Will it read mixed languages and numbers accurately?

One voice reads the entire script without automatic language switching. The voice interprets numbers, abbreviations, names, and mixed text, and may misread or skip them. Check by listening. This is not a pronunciation standard.

Is playback time my speech duration?

No. A voice's relative rate is not characters or words per minute and does not represent your live delivery. Use the speech length estimator as a planning reference, then time your own full rehearsal.

Can I pause, download, or keep playing with the screen locked?

This version has Play and Stop only. Play starts over after stopping. Moving to the background stops playback without automatic resumption. There is no download, recording, or voice cloning. Short gaps can occur between segments.

Is this Oralo voice following?

No. The preview reads text with a synthetic voice and does not listen to you. See the product pages for Oralo's iPhone and iPad prompting experience and features.
