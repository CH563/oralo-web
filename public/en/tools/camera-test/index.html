How to test

How to test

  1. Select “Start camera test” and allow camera access in your browser if asked.
  2. Check the local preview. If multiple cameras are available, select the one you intend to use. Turn on the mirrored preview if helpful.
  3. Adjust framing, background, and lighting. Select “Stop test”, then run a short test in your meeting or recording app.

The preview shows the camera picture received by this page. Check for motion, a covered lens, and suitable framing and background. This page does not automatically judge picture quality or call performance.

If there is no input or picture

Check the lens cover, physical privacy switch, connection, and selected camera. Then check site permission and system camera privacy settings. If needed, close apps that may be using it and retry. A black preview can have more than one cause.

Check again in the app you will use

After stopping this page, check the camera, framing, and output in the app you will use. Apps may crop, mirror, or process the picture differently. This preview does not guarantee their results.

Keep preparing your presentation

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio or video recorded, uploaded, or saved?

This page does not record audio or video, take snapshots, send media to us or third parties, or save it persistently. Live data is used only for the meter or preview. Browser and system services are outside this page's control. This is not a guarantee that your entire system operates offline.

Why is permission needed?

Browsers require permission before giving a page access to device input. After you select Start, this page requests only the permission needed for this tool. Saved permission may mean there is no new prompt. You can revoke permission in site settings.

What if access is denied or blocked by the system?

Check site permissions and system privacy settings, and allow this browser to access the relevant device. Settings vary by system and version. Return and retry manually after making changes. This page cannot change those settings for you.

Does this confirm Oralo or my meeting app will work?

No. Apps have their own permissions, device selection, and processing. Stop this page and test in the app you will use. This page does not check speech recognition or what another participant receives.

Can I use a phone or tablet?

Availability depends on the device, browser, permissions, and system restrictions. Try opening this page directly in an up-to-date Safari on iPhone or iPad, or Chrome on Android. This is not a compatibility guarantee for every model.

Why is an indicator still on after stopping?

This page stops its own media tracks, but hardware or browser indicators may update later. Another page or app may still be using the device. Stopping does not revoke saved site permission.

How do I test my webcam online?

Select “Start camera test”, allow access if asked, and check the preview. If several cameras are available, switch between them to compare framing. Stop when finished. This page does not need microphone permission.

Why is the preview black?

Check the lens cover, privacy switch, and selected camera first. Lighting, connection issues, system restrictions, or device use by another app may also be involved. Check and retry manually. A black preview alone does not identify the cause.

Can I switch front, rear, or external cameras?

Yes, when the browser exposes multiple selectable inputs. Some browsers do not expose every device, and names may be unavailable. Switching stops the previous preview first; this page does not keep multiple cameras active.

Does mirroring change what other people see?

Mirroring here changes the local preview only. It does not change camera output or control how another app displays or records it.

Is the displayed size the camera's maximum resolution?

No. It is the current track size reported by the browser. No number is shown if it is unavailable. Maximum camera specifications, sustained frame rate, and call output are different things and are not guaranteed here.
