The five-minute example

Chinese: floor((5 × 60 − 30) × 200 ÷ 60) = 900 characters.

English: floor((5 × 60 − 30) × 130 ÷ 60) = 585 words.

To reproduce a row, choose Time to word budget, set the language and time limit, enter the matching pace above, and set the extra allowance to 30 seconds. For mixed-language scripts, use Script to time, then time a full rehearsal.

01 / How is it calculated?

How is it calculated?

Divide Chinese characters by characters per minute and English words by words per minute. Add both times, then add your extra allowance. The final result is rounded to the nearest second.

Time in seconds

60 × H ÷ Rc + 60 × E ÷ Re + P

Character or word budget

floor((M × 60 − P) × R ÷ 60)H = Chinese characters; E = English words. Rc and Re are their respective per-minute paces. P = extra seconds; M = target minutes; R = the selected pace. Floor rounds the budget down.

A few useful answers

How many words fit a five-minute speech?

At 130 English words per minute with 30 seconds reserved, a five-minute speech has a budget of 585 words: floor((5 × 60 − 30) × 130 ÷ 60). At 200 Chinese characters per minute with the same allowance, the budget is 900 characters. Change these assumptions to match your delivery and time a full rehearsal.

How are Chinese and English counted?

Chinese is counted by character and English by word. Internal hyphens and apostrophes do not split a word. An abbreviation counts as one word. Counting Han characters is not language detection and is not suitable for Japanese scripts. This version does not support kana, Korean, or other writing systems.

Why write out numbers and symbols?

They can be read in different ways. The tool does not guess. Writing out what you plan to say makes the count better match your script.

Does punctuation add pause time?

Not automatically. Pace includes ordinary short pauses. Enter extra time for slide changes or interaction yourself. The tool does not add seconds for each punctuation mark.

Is my script uploaded or saved?

No. It is processed in the current page's memory, without upload or local storage. It is not retained after a refresh, language change, or leaving the page. Keep your own copy.

Is this Oralo voice following?

No. This page uses no microphone. It estimates timing from text counts. See the product pages for the iPhone and iPad app's prompting experience, and features.
