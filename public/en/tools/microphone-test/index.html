How to test

How to test

  1. Select “Start mic test” and allow microphone access in your browser if asked.
  2. Read the sample sentence at your normal volume and watch for changes in the meter. If multiple devices are available, select the microphone you intend to use.
  3. Select “Stop test”, then check the input device and run a short test in the app you will use.

A changing meter means the browser receives a changing sound signal. It may include background noise. It does not confirm clear speech, absence of distortion, or accurate recognition.

If there is no input or picture

Speak and check the headset's physical mute switch first. Then check the selected input, device connection, site permission, and system microphone privacy settings. If needed, close apps that may be using the device and retry manually.

Frequently asked questions

Is my audio or video recorded, uploaded, or saved?

This page does not record audio or video, take snapshots, send media to us or third parties, or save it persistently. Live data is used only for the meter or preview. Browser and system services are outside this page's control. This is not a guarantee that your entire system operates offline.

Why is permission needed?

Browsers require permission before giving a page access to device input. After you select Start, this page requests only the permission needed for this tool. Saved permission may mean there is no new prompt. You can revoke permission in site settings.

What if access is denied or blocked by the system?

Check site permissions and system privacy settings, and allow this browser to access the relevant device. Settings vary by system and version. Return and retry manually after making changes. This page cannot change those settings for you.

Does this confirm Oralo or my meeting app will work?

No. Apps have their own permissions, device selection, and processing. Stop this page and test in the app you will use. This page does not check speech recognition or what another participant receives.

Can I use a phone or tablet?

Availability depends on the device, browser, permissions, and system restrictions. Try opening this page directly in an up-to-date Safari on iPhone or iPad, or Chrome on Android. This is not a compatibility guarantee for every model.

Why is an indicator still on after stopping?

This page stops its own media tracks, but hardware or browser indicators may update later. Another page or app may still be using the device. Stopping does not revoke saved site permission.

How do I test my microphone online?

Select “Start mic test”, allow access if asked, and speak normally while watching the meter. Stop when finished. This page does not play your voice back.

Does a still meter mean my microphone is broken?

Not necessarily. Silence, physical mute, the wrong input, permissions, connections, or paused input analysis can affect the meter. Speak normally, then check these items. This page cannot diagnose hardware damage.

Why can I not hear myself?

This version does not route the microphone to speakers, to avoid echo and feedback. It does not record or offer playback. Watch the input meter. To check how you sound, use your intended app to record and review a short, non-sensitive sample.

Can this measure real-world decibels or sound quality?

No. The meter reflects relative changes in browser input, affected by device gain and system processing. It is not a calibrated sound-pressure meter and does not assess sound quality, noise reduction, or recognition accuracy.

Why are device names incomplete?

Browsers may hide names and some devices before permission, and may not expose every input afterward. Start with the default device and refresh the list. If selection remains limited, check browser or system input settings.
